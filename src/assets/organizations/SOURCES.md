# Organization mark sources

These marks match the organization names already used in `src/data/career.js`.
Each file is local to the site; the page does not load logos from these sources
at runtime.

- `jana.webp` — JANA Corporation's official logo shown on its [homepage](https://www.janacorporation.com/home-1), from its [Squarespace-hosted image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5eeb82a625b2cd6a78ffb911/1592499291873-1G5IYIFHSZWAJF70GRXY/jana_fl_fc.png?format=1500w). Encoded as a 512 × 162 lossless WebP.
- `sjtu.webp` — Shanghai Jiao Tong University's seal, cropped from the English lockup in the university's [official 130th-anniversary logo package](https://130.sjtu.edu.cn/en/service) (download: [PNG logo package](https://130.sjtu.edu.cn/storage/dwxcb/anniversary/en/file/2025/12/78b54bdb90d11870709ae5904835d012.rar)). The anniversary artwork was removed by cropping the existing seal; the mark was resized to 128 × 128 and encoded as lossless WebP.
- `utoronto.webp` — University of Toronto's official 48 × 48 favicon from its [homepage](https://www.utoronto.ca/) ([favicon source](https://www.utoronto.ca/themes/custom/bootstrap_uoft/images/favicons/favicon.ico)), converted to lossless WebP.

The JANA timeline uses the circular symbol at the left of its official lockup,
cropped with `object-fit` from the same local image. The SJTU seal keeps its
official shape and appears in red on a white holder in the research row.
